# Smooth combat movement

Melee positioning now searches a wider set of safe formation points and uses a short two-pulse lateral correction while the basic attack remains held. The recovery cadence keeps the correction smooth while continuing to bring more engaged targets into the forward attack cone.

The **Smooth side-step: keep targets in front** setting is enabled by default and remains subject to route safety, hunt boundaries, attack range, and group follow distance. Ranged attacks continue to use their existing positioning path.

# Smooth combat movement

Melee positioning now uses a short two-pulse lateral correction while the basic attack remains held. The smaller movement distance and recovery cadence reduce abrupt strafing while still bringing more engaged targets into the forward attack cone.

The **Smooth side-step: keep targets in front** setting is enabled by default and remains subject to route safety, hunt boundaries, attack range, and group follow distance. Ranged attacks continue to use their existing positioning path.
